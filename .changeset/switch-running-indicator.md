Two new components. `prism-switch` is a flat, wide on/off control with a pill thumb that stretches like a drop of liquid as it slides and springs back, while the accent washes across the track (about 240ms, none of it under reduced motion). It is a native `role="switch"` button that joins forms, with `label`, `checked`, `disabled`, `name`, `value`, `size` and `hide-label`, and it uses the shared state overlays. `prism-running-indicator` is a status dot with rings that spread outward and fade, in `sm`, `md` and `lg`, labelled for screen readers and still under reduced motion. Adds a Running job card recipe that puts the indicator in the card header, and the Resources overview recipe now shows a green indicator in place of the placeholder box, with a ghost "Online" tag.
